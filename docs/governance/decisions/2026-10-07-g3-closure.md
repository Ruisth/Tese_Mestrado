## 5. Qualifications retained by the approval

"With the qualifications of the Project Manager's opinion": the opinion named
in the header states that the decision must carry these four, transcribed here:

1. **Scope.** This is the functional P0 freeze on the documented ARM64 QEMU/TCG
   candidate, not native performance and not a complete experimental campaign.
2. **The sequence.** The standing results span the authorised sessions S1–S4,
   with the test 8 procedure repaired, test 6's instrumentation changed and
   later completions, on the same system under test. That disclosed sequence is
   accepted explicitly — the student's words: "Aceito a execução autorizada em
   S1–S4". It is **not** a literal single-pass execution of T1–T9 in order with
   identical tools, and nothing here says it was.
3. **Test 6.** Test 6 passes under the criterion adopted prospectively on
   2026-10-05 — the student's words: "a emenda prospetiva do T6". Its eventual
   6,720 exactly-once acceptances do not establish timely delivery at
   11.2 msg/s: 2,153 messages were accepted after the deadline (controller
   marker plus 60 s), the initial 67 late confirmations included and not
   added, and none was a permanent loss in that run. The historical invalid and
   incomplete outcomes and the analyser's global failed campaign checks stay
   unchanged.
4. **Defects and limits.** No open P0 defect was identified by the bounded
   review. The historical open risk rows and the deferred measurement of the
   `ditto-things` memory footprint remain disclosed; the evidence of no OOM is
   limited to these sessions. Tests 7, 8 and 9 keep their stated limits: one
   bounded fault of each dependency; persistence shown by event-directory names
   and identical twins, not by every file's bytes; the tested controls, not a
   security audit. No general stability, capacity, security-audit, C10/C12 or
   other claim is admitted (section 6).

"Preserving the historical results and the declared limitations": every earlier
attempt keeps its recorded class (section 7), and the limitations declared in
section 7 of the closing proposal and in the opinion are preserved as
disclosed limitations of its evidence — neither waived, nor marked solved, nor
accepted by implication; the sentence of the closing proposal's drafted row
that "the limitations of section 7 of this proposal are part of what is
accepted" is not adopted. The decision accepts what its words and the four
qualifications name; no further limitation or condition is read into it.
