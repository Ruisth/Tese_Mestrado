## 2. Accepted scope

Accepted, verbatim as the scope the approval covers:

> One bounded smartwatch → MQTT/TLS → controller → Ditto → API flow, and
> stored-twin persistence across one declared, quiescent whole-stack service
> restart, on the identified Yocto ARM64 guest **emulated with QEMU/TCG on an
> x86-64 host**.

What that covers, and nothing beyond it:

- **One smartwatch at 1 Hz for 60 seconds:** 60 valid messages confirmed within
  the controller-clock deadline, with no lost, late, duplicate or unaccounted
  record.
- **One fresh twin,** `org.c2dta:62da1188-4cd1-434b-a9c7-236a8c211f84`, run
  `itest-g2-01`, seed `20260921`, final sequence 59 and persisted accepted count
  60.
- **Persistence across the declared restart:** the same stored twin remains
  intact, with the queue quiescent and no intervening publication. The restart is
  evidenced by changed container and process identities; the per-process counters
  reset without loss of persisted twin state.
- **The preconditions of the run:** six services healthy, the recorded initial
  health and readiness with the counters at zero, the identified images and the
  demonstrated TLS publication path, as detailed in the reviewed proposal. As
  detailed there, the six services were observed healthy at the gate snapshot
  and again after the declared restart; these are point-in-time readings, and
  no interval of sustained health is claimed.

## 5. Qualifications retained by the approval

The approval is given "with the qualifications of the review". Those are the
four that the source record lists, and they are what the approval retains:

1. **Architecture of the five registry images** is established by the separately
   dated record of 2026-09-18 and by exact image-id and digest correspondence,
   **not** by architecture fields captured during the G2 session. A later changed
   image needs its own verification.
2. **Post-restart readiness** rests on the exit status of a hash-identified
   helper whose success requires HTTP 200. No response body was retained. This
   disclosed indirect evidence is accepted for this gate; future planned runs
   should capture the status and the body explicitly.
3. **Anonymous access was disabled in configuration.** A refusal was not
   exercised in this session, and no intentionally invalid payload was offered.
   Neither absence is presented as a passed negative test; exercised negative
   cases remain G3 work.
4. **Original attempts, including the failed preflight, retain their original
   bytes, timestamps, provenance and outcomes.** Post-session driver fixes are
   **not** credited as having run during the accepted session.

**Disclosed limitations that remain.** Section 6 of the
[acceptance proposal](../proposals/2026-09-21-g2-acceptance-proposal.md)
records sixteen residual limitations of the demonstration itself. The approval
does not mention them: they are not among the qualifications it retains, and
they are neither accepted nor waived by it. They remain disclosed limitations of
this evidence and are referenced here as such. Among them: one device of one
type; sixty messages at about one per second, an eleventh of the nominal
11.2 msg/s; one declared whole-stack restart with the queue empty, not a reboot,
a dependency fault or a restart under load; one guest, one session, one
repetition, so no reproducibility; an emulated platform on a shared host that
also carried the load generator; a simulated wearable, reconciliation helpers
and API reads that run on the host and reach the guest through port-forwards,
so that "inside the guest" describes the six services and the stored twin and
not the device; two sub-clauses that hold without being exercised; an image
architecture read on 2026-09-18; a controller image whose Python dependencies
are `UNLOCKED`; and health as a point-in-time reading, with no interval of
sustained health claimed.
