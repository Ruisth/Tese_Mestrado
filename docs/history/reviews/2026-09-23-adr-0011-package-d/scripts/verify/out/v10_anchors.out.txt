   275	**Shutdown.** To stop, the controller places one internal marker in the
   276	inbound queue; the marker is not a message. During shutdown `queue_depth`
   277	may therefore include that one marker, and the right-hand side of the
   278	identity may exceed `received` by exactly one. The meaning of `queue_depth`
   279	is not changed to hide it. While it is queued the marker occupies one queue

   281	before the configured capacity is reached; this is the existing shutdown
   282	behaviour, not a change. A message enqueued behind the marker is counted

   284	message in progress finishes normally and the backlog ahead of the marker is
