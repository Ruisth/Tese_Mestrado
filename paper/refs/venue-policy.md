# Venue policy and citation rules (paper)

The conference paper is **deferred until after the dissertation is submitted**
(plan section 4.5: writing it in parallel puts the dissertation at risk). This
file records the policy so it is settled before drafting starts.

## Citation policy

- Peer-reviewed records only; every entry verified against its primary source
  (publisher record or DOI resolution), never against a secondary list.
- DOIs required where one exists.
- The repository template asks to exclude preprint servers and certain
  publishers from the paper's bibliography. The dissertation's own bibliography
  follows the plan's rule instead (verified primary records), so the two
  bibliographies may legitimately differ: `paper/refs/references.bib` is a
  filtered subset, not a copy of `thesis/refs/references.bib`.

## Terminology — open question for the supervisor

The repository template asks for smart-devices/wearables terminology and to
avoid the word "telemetry". The dissertation and the implementation use
"telemetry" throughout, and it is fixed in normative interfaces that are cited
by tests and evidence (`src/CONTRACTS.md`: telemetry topic, telemetry envelope,
telemetry schemas). Renaming it in the artefact would invalidate archived
evidence.

Position to confirm before drafting the paper: keep "telemetry" in the
artefact and in the dissertation, and adopt the requested terminology in the
paper only. Recorded here rather than silently resolved either way.
